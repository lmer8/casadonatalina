Casa Donatalina

A place for people who live between languages.

Casa Donatalina is a space made of books, stories and conversations: a home, a bookshop, a café and a place to meet — even if, for now, only online.

A place to pause, read, browse, talk about books and meet other people who, like us, live between more than one language and more than one story.

Here you can come in to read an article, discover a book, leave a comment, listen to a story or simply stay for a while. And, if you wish, take a book home with you.

Who we are

Casa Donatalina was born from a family of readers, doctors, activists, storytellers and curious minds.

We believe in true stories.
In memory.
In books that continue to live even after the last page.

Here you will find novels, memoirs, book reviews and reflections on literature.
